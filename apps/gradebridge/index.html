    How GradeBridge works

    ExamSoft: Export assessment results as CSV with StudentID, First Name, Last Name, and raw points (Pts). GradeBridge does not support percentage-only exports.
    Brightspace: Export course grades as CSV with OrgDefinedId, First Name, and Last Name. Existing columns ending in “Points Grade” will be offered as grade-item choices.
    Do not open these CSV files in Excel before uploading them. Excel can alter student identifiers. If that happens, GradeBridge will stop and tell you to download a fresh Brightspace export.
    Saved courses: Reuse a roster for later exams when the class has not changed. GradeBridge warns when a saved roster is more than 60 days old.
    Final check: GradeBridge imports raw points. Before downloading, you must confirm the Brightspace grade item has the same maximum points as the assessment.
